1. The Triage Workflow

A recommended daily routine for staying on top of 800+ tasks across 20+ accounts.

  1. 1.
    Start at Morning Briefing — /briefing shows the top 50 red + orange tasks across all accounts, sorted by score. This is your "what needs attention today" view.
  2. 2.
    Use filter pills to narrow — "Ball with Me" shows tasks where you're the next to act. "Blockers" surfaces stuck tasks. "Stale 7d+" finds threads going cold.
  3. 3.
    Stack filters for precision — Filters combine with AND logic. "Ball with Me" + "Blockers" = blocked tasks that need your action.
  4. 4.
    Star important tasks — Press s to star a task and boost its score by +60 (applied when the task also has a qualifying signal such as a blocker, mention, approval request or staleness). Track starred tasks with the "Starred" filter.
  5. 5.
    Batch select for bulk actions — Press x to toggle selection mode. Select multiple tasks, then star/unstar all or open them in Basecamp.
  6. 6.
    Drill into account pages — Visit individual account pages for deeper per-client review. Use tier tabs (1-4) to filter by Urgent, Monitor, or Background tasks.

3. AI Analysis & Scoring

Every task gets a priority score from 0-100. The score combines deterministic signals (staleness, project type) with AI-detected signals (blockers, mentions) from Gemini analysis of the comment thread.

Priority Tiers

Critical (score 80-100) — Act now. Blocked, directly mentioned, or severely stale with multiple signals.
High (score 60-79) — Act today. Significant staleness, pending approvals, or important mentions.
Medium (score 35-59) — Check this week. Moderate staleness or pending actions.
Low (score 15-34) — Monitor. Some activity or minor signals, no immediate action needed.
Minimal (score 0-14) — Background. Recently active or waiting on client with no signals.

Score Breakdown Bar

The colored bar on each task shows how the score is composed. Each color represents a different signal:

Staleness — days since last comment
Joint project — 1.4x multiplier
Blocker / Deadline — blocked or due soon
Mention / Approval / Star — named or needs sign-off
Activity — 3+ comments (graduated)
Ambiguous — unclear ownership
Ball with producer — you hold the ball
Stage — workflow position

AI Signal Pills

These labels appear on task cards when Gemini detects specific conditions in the comment thread:

Blocker Something is preventing progress — a dependency, missing asset, or unresolved question.
Direct Mention You (or a monitored team member) were named in a recent comment.
Approval Needed Someone is waiting for sign-off, review, or a decision from the production team.
Ambiguous Unclear who should act next — the thread may need a nudge or clarification.

Ball With & Stage

Gemini determines who currently "holds the ball" (whose turn it is to act) and the workflow stage:

Ball with producer — You or your team need to take the next action (+20 to score).
Ball with client — Waiting on the client. Score is reduced (-10 for pending_client stage).
Stages: pending_review (+15), pending_client (-10), blocked (+5), in_progress (no change).

Star Boost

Starring a task adds +60 raw points (~20 on the 0-100 scale) to its score — but only when a qualifying condition exists (blocker, mention, approval, ambiguous ownership, pending review, or staleness >7 days). Starring a low-signal task won't inflate its score. The boost is part of the raw sum and interacts naturally with the joint multiplier.

Unassigned Tasks

Tasks with no assignee in Basecamp are hidden from all views. The poller continues tracking them, so when someone assigns the task in Basecamp it will automatically appear on the next poll cycle.

Staleness Badges

Days since the last comment on a task. Badges appear at 4+ days:

4-5d Getting stale
6-7d Needs attention
8-14d Overdue
15d+ Critical

4. Smart Filters

Filter pills appear on both the Account view and the Morning Briefing. Click to toggle; active filters glow.

Available Filters

Ball with Me Tasks where Gemini determined the ball is with you or your core team. These are your action items.
Blockers Tasks with a blocker detected — something is preventing progress.
Needs Approval Tasks where someone is waiting for a decision or sign-off.
Stale 7d+ Tasks with no comment activity in 7 or more days.
Starred Tasks you've manually starred for tracking.

How Filters Work

  • AND logic — each additional filter narrows the list. "Ball with Me" + "Blockers" = blocked tasks where you need to act.
  • Combine with tier tabs — on Account view, use tier tabs (1-4) plus filters. E.g., "Urgent" tab + "Ball with Me" = your highest-priority action items for that client.
  • URL-shareable — active filters appear in the query string (?filters=ball_with_me,blocker), so you can bookmark or share filtered views.

5. Upvote / Downvote Feedback

Thumbs up/down buttons on every task card let you flag whether a surfaced task was useful to see. This is purely a data-collection mechanism — votes do not affect scoring, visibility, or any other behavior.

How It Works

  • Upvote — marks the task as "useful to see." Use when the scoring system surfaced something you actually needed to act on.
  • Downvote — marks the task as "not useful." Use when a task was surfaced at a high priority but didn't warrant your attention.
  • Toggle — clicking the active vote again clears it. Clicking the opposite vote switches directly.
  • No scoring impact — votes are stored for future analysis only. They don't change the task's score or tier.

Where to Vote

  • Quick-action icons on task cards (Account view + Briefing)
  • "Upvote" / "Downvote" buttons in the task detail panel

Reviewing Votes

Use the "Upvoted" and "Downvoted" filter pills on Account or Briefing views to review tasks you've voted on. After accumulating votes over a few weeks, the data can be analyzed to identify patterns and inform future scoring refinements.
